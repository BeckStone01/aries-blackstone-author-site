From Chapter One · The Silent Beginning

From the moment of her birth, Ladonna Holloman defied expectations. Unlike other newborns, she never uttered a single cry.

Sound entered her before light did.

A rush of layered tones. Sharp. Rounded. Rising. Falling.

The first word she registered was not meaning, but pattern. The second was rhythm. The third was repetition.
