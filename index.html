About Aries Blackstone

Fiction where technology, pressure, and consequence collide.

Aries Blackstone writes fiction that blends advanced technology, political pressure, family loyalty, corporate ambition, and the human cost of innovation.

His stories center on brilliant characters facing impossible decisions in worlds shaped by power, secrecy, and consequence.
