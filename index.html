About Aries

Technology-driven fiction with power, pressure, and consequence.

Aries Blackstone is an American author, U.S. Air Force veteran, former military photographer, and longtime business owner. He writes technology-driven fiction centered on bold ideas, strategic tension, and characters navigating systems larger than themselves.

The Quantum Series introduces Ladonna Stone and Quantum Innovations, where ambition, secrecy, innovation, and conflict collide.
