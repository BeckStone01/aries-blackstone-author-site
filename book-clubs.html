Discussion Guide

Series-Level Questions

  1. When does Ladonna's responsibility become larger than her legal responsibility to Quantum Innovations?
  2. Does extraordinary intelligence create a greater moral obligation, or merely a greater ability to act?
  3. Which is more dangerous: the technology itself or the institutions and people competing to control it?
  4. When is secrecy justified when consequences extend beyond the organization that created the discovery?
  5. How should society respond when private technology develops faster than public regulation?
  6. Which characters confuse entitlement with merit?
  7. How does family change the meaning of risk for Ladonna?
  8. Where is the line between protecting innovation and monopolizing it?
  9. Which conflict feels most personal even when the stakes are institutional or global?
  10. Does the series portray government as safeguard, obstacle, necessary partner, or all three?
  11. How should technological benefits be distributed when one company controls the breakthrough?
  12. Which decisions appear defensible in the moment but create larger problems later?
  13. How does espionage dramatize ethical questions that would otherwise remain abstract?
  14. What would you do differently if you held Ladonna's authority and knowledge?
  15. Which question about technology and power remains unresolved?
