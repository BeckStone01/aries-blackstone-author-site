Quantum Edge: The Enigma of Ladonna Stone

5 out of 5 stars · Ams Strong for OnlineBookClub · November 11, 2023

When Ladonna takes charge of Quantum Innovations, Robert believes the board should have picked him. Strong’s review follows that rivalry and considers how differently the two characters respond when their authority is challenged.

“I enjoyed the character development in this book, the author’s selection of engaging words, and the fierce rivalry between Ladonna and Robert.”
