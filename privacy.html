Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Aries Blackstone author website, presented by Publishing Creative Creations, handles information from visitors.

Information You Choose To Send

If you contact us by email or request reader updates, we may receive your name, email address, message, and any other details you choose to include. We use that information to respond to your inquiry, send requested updates, and manage book, catalog, media, retailer, school, or library communications.

Newsletter And Reader Updates

This site uses MailerLite for reader-update signup forms and related newsletter functions. If you subscribe, information you provide is processed for requested reader updates and may be processed by MailerLite under its own privacy practices. You may unsubscribe using a newsletter link or request removal by emailing us.

External Links

This site links to third-party websites, including Amazon. Those websites are operated independently, and their privacy policies and terms apply when you visit them.

Cookies And Analytics

With your permission, this site uses Google Analytics 4 to measure visits and understand which public pages interest readers. If you choose Allow analytics, a Google Analytics script may set analytics cookies and send usage information such as page URLs, approximate location, browser details, and visit activity to Google. This information helps us improve the author website and evaluate outreach; we do not intentionally include names, email addresses, or other information you enter into forms in Google Analytics events. Google advertising personalization features are disabled for this website. See Google's Privacy Policy for information on Google's handling of data.

Google Analytics is optional. It is not loaded before you opt in, and declining does not limit use of the site. Your choice is saved in this browser. Use Analytics preferences near the bottom of any page to change it. Revoking permission stops future Google Analytics loading after the page refreshes; you can delete previously stored analytics cookies in your browser settings. Clearing site data also resets your choice. Visitor reports therefore describe measured, consenting visits, not necessarily every site visitor.

MailerLite and websites reached through links may handle information separately under their own policies.

Contact

For privacy questions or update requests, email info@publishingcreativecreations.com.
